Why Summit

Utilities already know batteries work. They want evidence.

The most valuable thing we build is not a battery. It is a data-rich, continuously operating platform that answers the questions every utility, grid operator and investor asks before committing.

Proven availability

Systems operating continuously, targeting greater than 98% availability, with every outage logged and explained.

Known round-trip efficiency

Measured, not modeled. Energy in and energy out recorded on every cycle, at the cell and at the AC connection.

Known degradation

Capacity and efficiency tracked over time so long-term performance can be forecast from real data.

Known maintenance cost

Every maintenance event, part and hour recorded, building a true picture of the cost to operate.

Remote monitoring

Circuit-level metering, SCADA dashboards and remote access across every system we operate.

Safe operation

Secondary containment, automatic shutdowns and fire and gas detection designed in from the start.

What we're building

Four systems, each one earning the next.

We scale step by step. Each system must prove itself before we invest in the next, larger one.

01

Commercial microgrid

A 20–30 kWh LFP battery microgrid with energy management, backup power and whole-site monitoring.

20–30 kWh LFP
02

Flow battery lab

Bench-scale iron and vanadium flow cells with full instrumentation to measure efficiency and validate stack designs.

Iron + vanadium
03

Field demonstrator

An outdoor-rated flow battery skid with SCADA and safety shutdowns, built for automated daily cycling.

1 kW / 10 kWh
04

Containerized unit

Stack, tanks, controls, inverter and monitoring integrated into a 20-ft container for demonstrations.

5 kW / 30–50 kWh

Our goal: a microgrid and a flow battery that have both cycled daily for 18 months, with every data point, outage, maintenance event and efficiency measurement on record.

Long-duration storage needs more than promising chemistry. It needs a track record. That is what we are building, one gated phase at a time, with the data to back it up.
