2027 Roadmap

One year. Five phases. Real data.

Our 2027 program moves from an operating microgrid to a field-operating flow battery, with formal go/no-go reviews before each larger step.

Gated approach

Each step has to earn the next.

Larger systems are only built once smaller ones meet defined performance and safety criteria.

G1Spring 2027

Microgrid baseline

Is the microgrid operating and the data platform reliable?

  • Utility permission to operate
  • 30+ days of data at ≥98% capture
  • Battery efficiency and load profile documented
G2May 2027

Can 75% efficiency be achieved?

The key technical decision of the program.

  • Bench iron cell round-trip efficiency at target
  • 100+ cycles with low capacity fade
  • Repeatable results across builds, zero leaks
G3Oct 2027

Demonstrator reliability

Is the field skid ready to scale up?

  • 30 consecutive days of automated cycling
  • ≥95% availability
  • No unresolved safety trips or leaks
G4Dec 2027

Year-end review

What have we proven, and what is next?

  • Microgrid availability above 98%
  • Known efficiency, degradation and maintenance cost
  • Demonstration package ready for partners
