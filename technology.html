Why flow batteries

Built for long duration and long life.

In a flow battery, energy is stored in liquid electrolyte held in external tanks and pumped through a cell stack. That design brings advantages for stationary storage.

Scalable energy

Power and energy sized separately

The stack sets the power; the tanks set the energy. Adding hours of storage means adding electrolyte, not more stacks.

Safety

Water-based electrolyte

Aqueous electrolytes are non-flammable, which supports safe operation near homes, businesses and critical facilities.

Materials

Iron as our primary path

We focus on iron chemistry, using abundant, low-cost materials, while testing vanadium at small scale for comparison.

Data & safety

Every system measured. Every system protected.

Data acquisition and SCADA

  • Target of at least 98% data capture from day one
  • Monthly review of availability, round-trip efficiency, degradation and maintenance for each system
  • Live SCADA dashboards and remote access
  • A maintenance and event log for every system

Safety by design

  • Secondary containment for all electrolyte
  • Safety review before any chemistry is purchased
  • Automatic shutdowns verified before unattended cycling
  • Fire and gas detection in enclosed systems
